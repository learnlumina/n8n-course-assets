# 50 Business Processes You Could Automate with n8n
### Life Inspired Learning

Use this list as a starting point. Pick 2–3 that would save you the most time and build those first.

---

## LEAD GENERATION & SALES

1. New form submission → add to CRM + send welcome email + notify sales team
2. New LinkedIn connection → send a connection message after 24 hours
3. Website contact form → score the lead with AI + route to right salesperson
4. Inbound email from new domain → research the company + create CRM record
5. New YouTube comment → check if it's a lead inquiry + notify you on Slack
6. Calendly booking → add to CRM + send meeting prep materials to prospect
7. Deal closed → trigger onboarding sequence automatically
8. Lead goes cold (no activity 14 days) → send a re-engagement email
9. Quote sent → follow up automatically if no response in 3 days
10. New referral received → thank the referrer + track in spreadsheet

---

## EMAIL & COMMUNICATION

11. Unread emails older than 48 hours → flag and notify yourself on Slack
12. Any email with "invoice" in subject → move to billing folder + log to sheet
13. New support email → AI drafts a reply → save as draft for review
14. Team @mentions in Slack → summarize at end of day and email to you
15. New email from VIP contact → send to phone via push notification
16. Weekly inbox summary → AI pulls key emails + emails you a digest every Monday
17. Email with attachment → save attachment to Google Drive automatically
18. Out-of-office detected in reply → reschedule follow-up for their return date
19. Email thread over 10 replies → AI summarizes the whole thread for you
20. New client email → create a Notion page for that client automatically

---

## CONTENT & SOCIAL MEDIA

21. New blog post published → auto-post teaser to LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook
22. New YouTube video published → generate a tweet thread from the transcript
23. RSS feed from industry news → summarize top 5 stories + send to Slack daily
24. New podcast episode → transcribe → generate show notes + social captions
25. Customer leaves a 5-star review → post to social media (with approval step)
26. Competitor publishes new content → alert you on Slack to review
27. Post gets high engagement → notify you + suggest a follow-up post
28. Content calendar spreadsheet updated → post scheduled content automatically
29. AI generates weekly content ideas → drops them into your Notion content board
30. New testimonial received → format as graphic brief + add to testimonials doc

---

## OPERATIONS & ADMIN

31. New invoice created → log to spreadsheet + set payment reminder at 30 days
32. Invoice unpaid at 7 days → send polite follow-up email automatically
33. Invoice unpaid at 14 days → AI drafts a firmer reminder + notifies you
34. New expense receipt in Gmail → extract amount + vendor + log to spreadsheet
35. Monthly recurring task → auto-create checklist in Notion or Asana
36. Team member submits time off request → update calendar + notify manager
37. End of day → summarize all completed tasks from project tool + email report
38. New vendor invoice → extract line items with AI + route for approval
39. Contract signed → start client onboarding sequence automatically
40. Project status changes → update all connected tools (CRM, Slack, Sheets) at once

---

## CUSTOMER SUCCESS

41. New customer signs up → create account folder + send welcome sequence
42. Customer hasn't logged in for 21 days → send a check-in email
43. Support ticket open for 48+ hours → escalate to senior team member
44. NPS score below 7 → alert customer success team immediately
45. Customer reaches usage milestone → send congratulations + upsell suggestion
46. Renewal coming in 60 days → start renewal conversation automatically
47. Customer asks a question in email → AI answers from your FAQ docs
48. New feature released → notify customers who requested it via email
49. Customer churn detected → trigger win-back sequence after 7 days
50. Annual review date approaching → schedule a call automatically

---

## HOW TO PICK YOUR FIRST AUTOMATIONS

Ask yourself:
- Which of these do I do manually more than once a week?
- Which one, if it broke or was missed, would hurt the business?
- Which one would impress my team or clients the most?

Start with one from Module 2 (simple, 3–4 nodes). Get it running. Then build the next one. By Module 7 you'll have 10 of these running and wonder how you ever did it by hand.
